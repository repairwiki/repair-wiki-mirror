## Authors

- Every file under `wikitext/`, `templates/` and `files/descriptions/` starts with a
  comment header naming the page's contributors (the usernames the wiki exposes
  through `prop=contributors`), the page's URL on the wiki, its history URL, a
  permalink to the mirrored revision and the licence.
- `contributors.json` lists every contributor with the number of pages they
  contributed to and their user page on the wiki.
- The wiki does not expose the IP addresses of anonymous editors. Pages with
  anonymous edits carry `anonymous_edits: N` in their header; the full history,
  including those edits, is at the page's `history` URL.
- Images carry their own credits in `files/index.json` (`uploader`, and `author`
  when the File: description page names one).
