# Nintendo Switch Not turning on at all Repair

## Problem description
No power at all
![Example image (Figure 1) -- No image yet. Help expand this page by uploading it!](images/3/30/Placeholder_image.jpg)
## Symptoms
- No power at all

## Solution
Could be various problems including M92T36, P13USB, BQ24193, battery fuel gauge IC (MAX17050) or MAX77620AEWJ and surrounding components. Check for continuity on coil near BQ24193 and fuse above port. Replace the faulty component.
