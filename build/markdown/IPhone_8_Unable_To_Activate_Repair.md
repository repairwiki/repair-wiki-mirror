# IPhone 8 Unable To Activate Repair

## Problem description
How to fix an iPhone 8 or iPhone 8 Plus that is stuck at the Hello screen.
![Example image (Figure 1) -- No image yet. Help expand this page by uploading it!](images/3/30/Placeholder_image.jpg)
## Symptoms
- After the phone has been restored it gets stuck at the Hello screen.
- You try to activate and get the "Unable To Activate" screen.

## Solution
There are 2 potential causes

1. Baseband issue. See [IPhone 8 Stuck in Searching or No Service Repair](IPhone_8_Stuck_in_Searching_or_No_Service_Repair.md) for solutions.
1. NFC issue. You most likely need to reball or replace NFC_RF.
