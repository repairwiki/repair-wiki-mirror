# Xbox Elite Controller Series 2 RB or LB is only registering clicks when certain parts of the bumper are pressed or when the button is pressed with too much force repair

## Problem description
RB or LB is only registering clicks when certain parts of the bumper are pressed or when the button is pressed with too much force.
![Example image (Figure 1) -- No image yet. Help expand this page by uploading it!](images/3/30/Placeholder_image.jpg)
## Symptoms
- RB or LB is only registering clicks when certain parts of the bumper are pressed or when the button is pressed with too much force.

## Solution
Replace the RB or LB switch on the blue board.
