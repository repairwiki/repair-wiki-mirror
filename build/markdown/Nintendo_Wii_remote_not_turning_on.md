# Nintendo Wii remote not turning on

## Problem description
Controller does not turn on
## Symptoms
- Nopower on the remote

## Solution
- Check the batteries in a Known good device such as a wireless mouse, etc.

Check Battery Contacts.

If device is not taking power:

- open remote.

- check continuity as follows

![If it is unreliable, or if there is battery acid on the plate, remove the spring from the plate, and put it in a small container of white vinegar.

If there is a large amount of acid on the plate, it might be best to remove the plate and put it in white vinegar.

Resolder the plate back onto the logic board.

Test.
