## More Information/External Sources
Manufacture: https://walksnail.com/

The Avatar VTX is designed to be used with the Fat Shark Dominator, Walksnail Avatar HD FPV Goggles and Caddx Goggles X.
- Lower latency than the competitor at an average of 22ms
- Built-in 8Gb Internal Storage
- 1080p Screen Resolution
- Switchable Aspect Ratio
- Dual Antenna Design
- 5.8GHz Frequency
- Onboard DVR
- Canvas Mode
- 4km Range
- 170° FOV
- 6V-25.2V
