# Xbox Series X No Video Repair

## Problem description
No Display / No Video Output
![Pinout for bypassing the ESD Protection IC](images/c/cc/Screen_Shot_2022-09-16_at_8.02.54_PM.jpg)

## Symptoms
- No Display / No Video Output

## Solution
- Put your Xbox into low resolution mode.  [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4QTy-ILtqyc Low Resolution mode demonstration. ￼]
- Replace HDMI Cable
- Inspect HDMI Port for Damage
- Check EMI Filters Below Port
- Check HDMI Encoder (Possibly Not Available to Purchase)
- Check ESD Protection IC (Underside of Motherboard) - You could also bypass the ESD Protection IC by using the pinout shown. To be used for diagnostic purposes.
- Check 2151 5v Transistor. Can Be Replaced with Similar Transistor from OG Xbox One Ethernet Circuit

There's a video [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fKjBFbANsXI on YouTube] which goes over the fault-finding process for no display when the port is not to blame. There's also a video on [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ehf2nt9W5iU YouTube] which goes over the replacement process for Xbox Series X HDMI ports.

Here is another [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEGnK0XnP00&t=173s YouTube] video for bypassing the ESD Protection IC.
