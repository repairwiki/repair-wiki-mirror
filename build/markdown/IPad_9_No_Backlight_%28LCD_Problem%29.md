# IPad 9 No Backlight (LCD Problem)

## Problem description
The LCD of iPad Produces no backlight.
- == Solution ==

  - This Guide is only made to repair the LCD screen.
  - The LCD of this iPad has a defect on the Backlight cable that can be repaired without swapping the backlight module.

First remove this sticker.
![Sticker to remove.png](images/7/71/Sticker_to_remove.png)
Next remove this piece of metal.
![Piece of metal to remove.png](images/2/2f/Piece_of_metal_to_remove.png)
Next using a small precision dremel with a cutting bit make a small hole like this.

Be careful to not cut too deep or you will damage the LCD, cut really slow.
![Hole.png](images/e/e3/Hole.png)
After making the hole you will see the cable is ripped in this section.
![Ripped cable.png](images/a/a4/Ripped_cable.png)
To repair the cable we will need to expose the coper traces,use a X-Ato blade like this one.
![X-acto.png](images/0/01/X-acto.png)
Scrape the cable very gently until it looks like this.
![Cable.png](images/4/49/Cable.png)
After scraping we need to do a few jumpers like the image bellow.

  - Avoid using to much flux because if you do it will infiltrate the LCD and you will have stains on LCD**
![Jumpers.png](images/0/07/Jumpers.png)
After doing the jumps you can place a small piece of kapton tape over it.

After doing this test the LCD and you should have it back working like normal.
![Repaired iPad.png](images/4/4c/Repaired_iPad.png)
