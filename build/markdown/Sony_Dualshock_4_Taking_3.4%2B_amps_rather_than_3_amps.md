# Sony Dualshock 4 Taking 3.4+ amps rather than 3 amps

## Problem description
Device does not turn on
![Example image (Figure 1) -- No image yet. Help expand this page by uploading it!](images/3/30/Placeholder_image.jpg)
## Symptoms
- Taking 3.4+ amps rather than 3 amps

## Solution
- You have the wrong ribbon cable plugged in; there are 10, 12, and 14 pin ribbon cables depending on your controller version. Google the charger's name (e.g. JDS-030 or JDS-011) and find out how many pins you need. Either solder a wire to every connection or buy a new ribbon cable
