# Nintendo Switch No fan spin and not charging joycons Repair

## Problem description
No fan spin and not charging joy-cons
![Example image (Figure 1) -- No image yet. Help expand this page by uploading it!](images/3/30/Placeholder_image.jpg)
## Symptoms
- No fan spin
- not charging joy-cons

## Solution
- Issues with fan circuit. You can use a voltage injection trick where you briefly inject voltage into the fan (in circuit) to bring the mosfet back to life. [https://repair.wiki/w/Nintendo_Switch_No_Fan_Spin_Voltage_Injection_Method Here's A Guide]. If voltage injection doesn't work, replace the mosfet above M92T36.

- Issue can also be caused by a bad CPU fan.
