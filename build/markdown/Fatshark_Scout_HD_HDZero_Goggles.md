## More Information/External Sources
Fat Shark Scout HD FPV Goggles For HDZero (AKA Shark Byte) Digital FPV Goggles manufactured by Fat Shark before they ended manufacturing HD Zero systems.

The Scout HD are the first digital FPV goggles to be sold with HDZero built in, up to Febuary 2022 these have only been sold are PVT or “Beta” products according to Fat Shark.

Based on HDZero Technology http://www.hd-zero.com/

In 2021 Fat Shark stopped manufacturing HD Zero based system and HDZero them selves continued development.
