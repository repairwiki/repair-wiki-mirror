# Playstation 4

The PlayStation 4 (commonly referred to as PS4) is a home video game console developed by Sony. It is the successor to its predecessor, the PlayStation 3. It was designed to compete with Microsoft's Xbox One and Nintendo's Wii U and Switch in the eight generation of video game consoles.
