# Steam Deck Mainboard Testing Points

Testing Points of the Steam Deck Mainboard are shown in Figure 1. Values are shown in Table 1. Testing points are divided by sectors ("Position"). Measurements were done with a VC99 (Aliexpress clone) Multimeter. Measurements in diode mode were done with the positive terminal of the multimeter connected to ground of the mainboard (housing of the battery connector) and the probe was connected to the COM terminal of the multimeter. Resistance measurements were done in regular configuration.
