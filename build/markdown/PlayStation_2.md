# PlayStation 2

The PlayStation 2 (commonly referred to as PS2) is a home video game console developed by [Sony Computer Entertainment](Sony_Computer_Entertainment.md). As a sixth-generation console, it competed with Microsoft's Xbox, Nintendo's [GameCube](GameCube.md), and Sega's [Dreamcast](Dreamcast.md). It is the best selling video game console of all time, selling over 160 million units worldwide, tripling the combined sales of its competing consoles.
