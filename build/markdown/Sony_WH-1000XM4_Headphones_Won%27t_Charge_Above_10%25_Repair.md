# Sony WH-1000XM4 Headphones Won't Charge Above 10% Repair

## Problem description
How to repair Sony WH-1000XM4 headphones that won't fully charge the battery.
![Example image (Figure 1) -- No image yet. Help expand this page by uploading it!](images/3/30/Placeholder_image.jpg)
## Symptoms
- Headphones won't charge past 10%

## Solution
Replace SUB_R board (aka right side board / the PCB in the right speaker assembly.) This is one of the most common issues with the WH-1000XM4.
