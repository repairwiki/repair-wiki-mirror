# How to fix an iPad Air that shows the low battery screen but will not charge

## Problem description
Fixing an issue where the iPad responds to a charger being plugged in by showing the low battery screen but will not actually charge the battery
![Example image (Figure 1) -- No image yet. Help expand this page by uploading it!](images/3/30/Placeholder_image.jpg)
## Symptoms
- Battery is too low to power on the iPad.
- Screen goes blank as soon as the charger is disconnected.

## Solution
This is most commonly caused by an old or defective battery that needs to be replaced.
