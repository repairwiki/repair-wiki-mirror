# Zenimals Sound Machine

Zenimals is a sleep sound machine. It provides meditations and white noise to help one get to sleep. Zenimal sleep "collections" (audio tracks) are delivered in a proprietary format (on microsd cards) and the device will only play proprietary audio. There is no way for users to play their own audio or repair failed microsd cards.
