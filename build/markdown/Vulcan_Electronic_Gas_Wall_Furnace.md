# Vulcan Electronic Gas Wall Furnace

The **Vulcan Electronic Gas Wall Furnace** is a natural gas wall furnace with a front electronic control panel and fan outlet grill at floor level.

Different furnaces with similar operation or internals can be found under the brand Bonaire or Pyrox.

The internal control PCB may be called either "EFD Ignition Unit" or "Ignitor Module" as part number E.0.501104, 501EFD or 470120
