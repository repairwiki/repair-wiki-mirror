# How To Replace iPhone Front Camera Without Loosing FACE ID

## Problem description
Starting from iPhone X, Apple introduced the TrueDepth camera system, which integrates several sensors responsible for Face ID, ambient light detection, and proximity functions.

Because these components are serially paired to the logic board, replacing the entire assembly will permanently disable Face ID.

However, if only the front camera module (not the Dot Projector or IR Camera) is damaged, it can be replaced without losing Face ID — provided the original TrueDepth sensors remain intact and untouched.
![Example image (Figure 1) -- Cutting Metal Shield Around Front Camera](images/6/64/Cutting-shield.png)

## Symptoms
- Front camera not working or showing black screen in Camera app.
- Face ID still functions normally before repair.
- No liquid damage or board-level fault detected.
- TrueDepth assembly intact but camera lens or module defective.

## Solution
To replace only the front camera module within the TrueDepth assembly:

1. Retain the original Dot Projector and IR Camera — they are paired to the logic board.
1. Cut open the metal cage around the front camera.
1. Remove and replace the front camera module only.
1. Reassemble carefully, ensuring no damage to the Dot Projector or IR Camera.

When done properly, Face ID will continue to function normally, and the new camera will work for photo/video.

  - NOTE**: Sometimes when the Front Camera doesn't work after replacement, It usually requires an update to be flashed for the camera to work.
![Example (Figure 2) -- iPhone 11 Pro Camera After Removing From TrueDepth Camera Assembly](images/2/21/Front-cam-only.png)

### Diagnostic Steps
1. Test Before Disassembly
1. * Check Face ID functionality in Settings.
1. * Open Camera → test front and rear cameras.
1. * If only front camera fails, but Face ID remains functional, proceed.
1. Inspect TrueDepth Assembly
1. * Verify no liquid or corrosion on the front flex or connectors.
1. * Confirm that the IR Camera and Dot Projector lenses are clean and undamaged.

### Repair Steps
1. Preparation
1. * Power off device and disconnect the battery.
1. * Remove the display and access the TrueDepth assembly.
1. Expose the Front Camera
1. * Locate the metal shield covering the front camera on the TrueDepth module.
1. * Use a mini-dremel or precision cutter to carefully slice open the metal shield — do not apply heat directly to avoid sensor damage.
1. Remove the Damaged Camera
1. * Heat the camera slightly and lift out the front camera module using tweezers.
1. Install New Camera
1. * Align the replacement camera into the same position.
1. * Ensure alignment matches original orientation.
1. * Re-secure the area with a thin adhesive or Kapton tape if needed.
1. Reassembly & Testing
1. * Reinstall display, reconnect all flexes, and power on.
1. * Test:
1. ** Front camera photo/video
1. ** Portrait mode
1. ** Face ID setup and unlock
1. ** Proximity during calls  If all functions pass — Face ID is retained successfully.
NOTE:  If front camera is not working after replacing, try flashing an update with iTunes or 3UTools
