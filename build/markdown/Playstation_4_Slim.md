# Playstation 4 Slim

The PlayStation 4 Slim is a hardware revision of the original PlayStation 4 developed by Sony. The "Slim" version is widely regarded as the better choice for most users due to its energy efficiency, reduced noise level, affordability, HDR support and its updated controllers.
