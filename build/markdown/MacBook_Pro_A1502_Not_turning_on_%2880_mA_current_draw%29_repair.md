## Symptoms
- Not turning on.
- Only drawing 80 mA from the charge port.

## Solution
Corrosion at U1900 RTC (Real Time Clock) circuit

- U1900 and C1910 were removed
- Corrosion was removed
- U1900 and C1910 were restored.
