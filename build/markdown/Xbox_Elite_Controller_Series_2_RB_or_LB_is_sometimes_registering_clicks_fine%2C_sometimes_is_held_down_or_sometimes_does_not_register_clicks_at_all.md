# Xbox Elite Controller Series 2 RB or LB is sometimes registering clicks fine, sometimes is held down or sometimes does not register clicks at all

## Problem description
RB or LB is sometimes registering clicks fine, sometimes is held down or sometimes does not register clicks at all.
![Example image (Figure 1) -- No image yet. Help expand this page by uploading it!](images/3/30/Placeholder_image.jpg)
## Symptoms
- RB or LB is sometimes registering clicks fine, sometimes is held down or sometimes does not register clicks at all.

## Solution
Most likely the actual switches are working fine. Replace the chip marked "NXP L16A" (Full name: PCAL6416AHF) on the blue board.
